SBGN · process description

Beta-carotene splits in two

Six cleavage processes in cytosol split seven carotenoids. Beta-carotene is cut into B-10-apo and B-ionone; cryptoxanthin and zeaxanthin feed the same product pools.

THE HEADLINE

Beta-carotene splits in two

v1 consumes B-carotene and produces both B-10-apo and B-ionone. The two fragments are the story, not a leftover.

What feeds the pools

  • B-crypto via v3 and v4
  • Zeaxanthin via v6
  • OH-B-10 continues to OH-B-ionone

Squares are reactions

Species never connect to species. Every cleavage is a process node, so consumption and production stay distinct.

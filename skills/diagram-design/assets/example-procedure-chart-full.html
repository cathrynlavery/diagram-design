THE HEADLINE

The wait is the procedure

EXP-001-05 is a delay symbol: the claim sat for a day. Every other charted step takes minutes.

Two tracks, one chart

  • Personnel symbols say what a role does, present tense
  • Paperwork symbols say what happened to the form, past tense
  • Each handoff starts at a move symbol and names both columns

A diamond is an inspection

On a procedure chart the diamond means a mental verification, not a branch. Decision logic belongs in a flowchart, with its own legend.

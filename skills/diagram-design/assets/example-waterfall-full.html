How a 240 $k/mo baseline becomes a 236 $k/mo plan. Two costs rise, two fall — and the running total carries across every gap, so the walk has to reconcile.

LARGEST SAVING

Storage tiering · −52

Moving cold datasets to archive tiers claws back more than storage grew all year. It lands late in the walk on purpose — the carry shows the budget was above baseline until this one step.

The walk reconciles

240 + 64 − 38 + 22 − 52 = 236. Every bridge spans exactly its two running levels, and each carry line transports the total across the gap at that level — the chart is checkable arithmetic, not decoration.

What the bridge doesn't show

Each bar is a net figure. Headcount's +64 contains both new hires and one team's downsizing; the walk states contributions, not the churn inside them.

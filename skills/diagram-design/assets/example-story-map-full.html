Reporting, first release

Narrative order runs left to right — find the data, build the report, share it, trust it — while priority runs top to bottom. The release-cut line under MVP is the editorial point: everything above it ships first, and the riskiest bet, row-level permissions, lands in Release 2.

RELEASE CUT

MVP · 4 stories

Keyword search, a table with one chart, a public link, and a last-updated stamp — enough to answer "what is the whole story" without shipping every activity's full depth. Everything below the cut line waits.

Release 2 · 3 stories

Saved filters and scheduled email round out the workflow, but row-level permissions is the riskiest bet in the map — untested access-control logic sitting right after the cut, not buried in "Later".

Later · 2 stories

Natural-language query and anomaly alerting are named and ordered, not promised — three slices is the ceiling before nobody believes the sequencing anymore.

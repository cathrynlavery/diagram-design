CI compute budget · a month of pipeline minutes

12,000 CI minutes split across four pipeline stages, then merge into three outcomes. Band thickness is the data — the flaky-rerun path is the only colored ribbon, because it's the line item worth cutting.

EDITORIAL POINT

Flaky reruns

1,000 of the 12,000 monthly minutes — about 8% of the whole budget — go to tests that failed once and passed on rerun. That's the one line item this diagram argues for cutting.

Where the minutes go

Unit tests and E2E together are three-quarters of the budget (9,200 of 12,000 minutes). Build and lint are comparatively cheap at 1,900 and 900.

Net outcome

9,400 minutes pass clean, 1,600 fail for real. The remaining 1,000 are pure overhead — neither signal nor failure, just retries.

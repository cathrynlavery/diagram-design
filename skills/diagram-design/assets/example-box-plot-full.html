Box plot · Diagram Design

Checkout's box is the incident

Five services, one shared latency axis from zero. Auth and Search keep a tight box under 40 ms. Checkout's box already runs 80–200 ms, and two requests sit past 380 ms — the median still looks survivable, which is exactly why the box is the reading that matters.

The box, not the middle

Checkout pages on the IQR

Checkout's median is 120 ms. Its box already spans 80–200 ms, and two outliers sit at 388 and 396 ms. A single average would have called this service healthy. The five-number summary is the incident.

One shared scale

Zero to 400 ms, linear

Every box, median, whisker and outlier sits on one linear axis from zero. Auth's entire box fits inside Checkout's lower whisker. That comparison is the point of putting five services on one figure.

Outliers stay outside

Dots past the fence

Whiskers run to the last inlier, not to the theoretical fence, and the two Checkout requests past its 380 ms fence keep their own marks. Drawing them on the whisker would have hidden the tail the title is about.

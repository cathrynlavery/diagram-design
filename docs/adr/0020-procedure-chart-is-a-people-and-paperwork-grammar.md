# ADR 0020 — Procedure chart is a people-and-paperwork grammar

**Status:** proposed

## Context

Methods and records studies chart an administrative procedure two ways at once: what each person does, and what happens to each piece of paper as it is created, carried, left idle, checked, copied from, filed or destroyed. AFMAN 38-102 (5 July 2024) §11.3, "Preparing a Procedure Chart", sets out the layout. Its Figure 11.4, "Common Procedure Chart Symbols and Usage", defines eleven paperwork symbols and three personnel symbols. Both were read from the primary publication for this record; nothing here is reconstructed from a secondary description.

ADR 0002 sets the bar for growth: a new type needs a genuinely new layout grammar. The nearest existing types were audited against it:

- **Swimlane** gives each actor a lane and draws steps with arrows. It has no document lines, so it cannot show one form travelling through three offices. It also has no symbols for delay, file, inspection or destruction.
- **Flowchart** draws decision logic. Its diamond means *decision*. On a procedure chart the same diamond means *inspection*, a mental verification with one way out. Sharing a legend would make one shape mean two things.
- **Process** shows payload handoffs between actors. It has no fixed personnel track and no per-document line.
- **Service blueprint** splits frontstage from backstage on a stage grid. It has no document lifecycle.
- **Traceable block decomposition** (ADR 0010) gives stable, citable IDs, but it decomposes structure on a Tree. A procedure is a sequence.

None of these forces all four constraints that make the figure: (a) one personnel column on the left, kept apart from paperwork; (b) one line per document across organizational columns; (c) a column change only at a move symbol with a labelled handoff; (d) symbol shapes with Figure 11.4 meanings, inspection diamond included.

## Decision

Add **Procedure chart** as visual type 53 with the full §10 shipping set:

- `references/type-procedure-chart.md`;
- three variants of one fictional expense-claim procedure, generated by `scripts/build-procedure-chart-examples.py` so they cannot drift;
- a gallery tab, a selection-table row and frontmatter hook, and a budget row;
- the canonical screenshot;
- an executable contract, `scripts/verify-procedure-chart.py`, with adversarial tests in `scripts/test-verify-procedure-chart.py`.

Scoped decisions:

- **Opt-in by routing.** Nothing changes for existing diagrams. The verifier ignores files without `data-diagram="procedure-chart"`, except to reject `data-proc-*` vocabulary leaking into them.
- **One legend, one meaning per shape.** A procedure chart legend may not name a decision, and a non-inspection activity may not draw a diamond. Flowchart keeps its diamond and its meaning. A procedure that also needs branching logic gets a companion flowchart.
- **Reuse the block registry, add a connector registry.** The procedure, its artifacts and its activities are `data-block-*` blocks, so `--registry` export and `verify-block-registry.py` cover them unchanged. Lines are not blocks. Flow lines and handoffs are their own registry (`data-proc-connector`, `data-proc-connector-id`, `data-from`, `data-to`), because a connector is a relation between blocks, not a part of the tree.
- **Declared time, illustrative trace.** Processing time is a declared attribute per document symbol, and the header's charted time must equal their sum. An optional event trace shows one imagined run. It lives in its own `data-role="trace"` group, never carries block IDs, and must say it is illustrative. The skill draws procedures; it does not collect employee or document telemetry.
- **Personnel subset.** The personnel track allows only operation, inspection and move, as Figure 11.4 does.
- **Static only.** Per ADR 0001 there is no animated form.

## Rejected, with reason

- **A semantic pattern on Swimlane.** A pattern may add primitives but not a second layout grammar (ADR 0002). Per-document lines, the fixed personnel track and the handoff-at-a-move rule are layout, not behaviour.
- **Reusing Flowchart's shapes with a different legend.** The diamond collision is the reason this type exists. Two charts in one document would teach the reader two meanings for one shape.
- **Hand-authored examples.** Three variants with sixteen activities each drift easily. Generation plus `--check` keeps them identical except for palette and frame, as the exploded-axonometric builder does.
- **Claims of AFMAN compliance.** The type borrows the symbol set and the layout rules. It makes no claim that a chart meets any manpower-study requirement.

## Consequences

- The verifiable count moves to 53 in `verify-docs-sync.py`, `verify-semantic-motion.py`, the screenshot catalog scripts and ADR 0002's amendment log.
- `type-flowchart.md` and `type-swimlane.md` route procedure requests here, so the router stays unambiguous.
- `data-proc-*` is new vocabulary. It follows the precedent of other type-scoped attribute families, each with a validator that reads only its own attributes.
